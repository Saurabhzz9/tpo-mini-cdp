This project uses human-directed engineering with AI-assisted implementation. Engineering ownership includes deciding what to build, choosing constraints, reviewing behavior and accepting trade-offs; it is not measured solely by who enters source text.

The developer specifically reports active participation in system design and technology trade-offs, feature review, database/identity requirements, privacy/security review, generated-source review, issue identification, manual browser testing, E2E direction and review, fix evaluation, deployment planning and technical acceptance. These are engineering contributions. They are retained as developer-reported where the session contains no independent review/test record.

The assistant's recorded automated work is evidence of those particular executions, not evidence that a human independently performed them. Conversely, AI-generated implementation does not erase the developer's engineering direction or ownership. An exact handwritten-line percentage cannot be responsibly inferred from commit style or author metadata. The authorship of the initial brief is irrelevant to this distinction and is not inferred.
